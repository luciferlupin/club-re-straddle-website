CLUB RE STRADDLE CARD ROOM CODE & REGULATIONS

To ensure a world-class, transparent, and fair gaming atmosphere for all members and guests, Club Re Straddle enforces the following official room rules across all cash tables and tournaments:

1. Player Eligibility & KYC

All members must be 18+ years of age with government-issued photo ID verification. Club Re Straddle strictly maintains a registered member-only policy.

2. Table Stakes Policy

Only chips currently on the table in plain view before a hand begins may be wagered. Removing chips from the table (ratholing) is strictly prohibited.

3. One Player Per Hand

No table talk regarding active hands, folded cards, or collusion is tolerated. Every player must make decisions entirely independently.

4. Floor Manager Decisions

The Floor Manager's ruling is final in all disputes. Management reserves the right to make decisions in the interest of fairness and game integrity.

5. String Bets & Forward Motion

Verbal declarations (Call, Raise, All-in) are binding. Forward motion with chips towards the pot constitutes an intentional bet in that amount.

6. Club Etiquette & Hospitality

Respectful conduct towards fellow players, professional dealers, and club staff is mandatory. Abusive language will lead to immediate revocation of membership.

Club Re Straddle is the premier legal poker club in NCR Noida. Experience top-tier tournament action and hospitality.
