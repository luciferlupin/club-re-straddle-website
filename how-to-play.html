BEGINNER'S GUIDE

LEARN HOW TO PLAY POKER OFFLINE

Poker is one of the most exciting card games, whether played offline with friends or offline for real rewards. While casual home games often come with custom or flexible house rules, offline club poker at Club Re Straddle sticks strictly to official international tournament and cash game standards, making the game competitive, fair, and thrilling.

Playing by the real rules sharpens your skills and elevates the excitement of reading players and testing your mettle. At Club Re Straddle, you play live poker with passionate, real players who share an authentic love for the game.

Poker is typically played with 2 to 9 or 10 players at a single table using a standard deck of 52 cards. Each hand progresses through distinct betting stages until the winning hand is revealed at showdown or all other opponents fold.

STEP-BY-STEP

THE 4 BETTING ROUNDS OF TEXAS HOLD'EM

01

1. Pre-Flop

Each player receives 2 private hole cards. Players post Small & Big Blinds. The first round of betting begins starting left of the Big Blind.

02

2. The Flop

The dealer burns one card and deals the first 3 community cards face up on the felt. A new betting round commences.

03

3. The Turn

A 4th community card is dealt face up. Players analyze potential flushes, straights, and sets. Third round of betting occurs.

04

4. The River

The 5th and final board card is revealed. The final betting round takes place followed by Showdown where the highest hand wins the pot.
