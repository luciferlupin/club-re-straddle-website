EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW

Here are common inquiries about our club operations, memberships, games, food packages, and legal protections.

Is playing poker at Club Re Straddle legal in Noida NCR?

Yes, absolutely. The Supreme Court of India and several High Courts (including Allahabad HC and Karnataka HC) have judicially established that Poker is a game of skill. As a skill-based recreational game, playing poker is legally protected under Article 19(1)(g) of the Constitution of India. Club Re Straddle operates with all requisite compliance and certifications.

What are the opening hours of the club?

Club Re Straddle is open everyday. Cash tables and private games run 24 hours with dedicated dealer shifts, concierge support, and food service.

What game variants and stakes are hosted?

We host Texas Hold'em (NLHE), Pot Limit Omaha (PLO-4 and PLO-5), and tournament series. Stakes range from casual micro/low stakes (₹25/₹50, ₹50/₹100) to mid & high stakes (₹100/₹200, ₹200/₹500, ₹500/₹1000) with optional Straddle and Re-Straddles.

Can I book a private table for family and friends?

Yes! We offer exclusive Table + Food Packages designed for groups of friends, family gatherings, corporate celebrations, or private birthday tourneys. Dedicated private rooms with professional dealers and gourmet snacks are provided.

How do I register or join the club?

Simply reach out via our Contact page or connect with our membership concierge directly on WhatsApp. You must provide a valid government ID (Aadhaar/Passport/Driving License) upon your first arrival.

Where is Club Re Straddle located?

We are conveniently located at JB Complex, Sector 104, Noida, Uttar Pradesh — easily accessible via Noida Expressway with valet parking available.

Club Re Straddle is the premier legal live poker club in Delhi NCR & Noida. Delivering certified fair play, international-grade croupiers, and world-class luxury poker action.
