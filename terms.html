HOME TERMS

1. Acceptance of Terms

By visiting, registering, or participating in card room games and tournaments at Club Re Straddle (JB Complex, Sector 104 Noida), you agree to comply with and be bound by these Terms and Conditions.

2. Age & Identification

Participation is strictly restricted to individuals who are 18 years of age or older. Valid government identification (Aadhaar, Passport, Voter ID, Driving License) is required for entry and membership verification.

3. Club Etiquette & Integrity

Club Re Straddle reserves the right to refuse service or revoke membership of any individual engaging in collusive behavior, table misconduct, abuse of floor staff, or non-compliance with floor manager decisions. All games adhere to standard International TDA (Tournament Directors Association) and Robert's Rules of Poker.

4. Game of Skill Protection

Poker is recognized under Indian jurisprudence as a game preponderantly reliant on skill, judgment, and mathematical acumen, protected under Article 19(1)(g) of the Constitution of India. All sessions are structured strictly in compliance with applicable laws.
