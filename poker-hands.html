HAND HIERARCHY

OFFICIAL POKER HAND RANKINGS (HIGHEST TO LOWEST)

To master poker at Club Re Straddle, you must know the exact hierarchy of poker hands. Here is the definitive ranking of five-card hands from strongest to weakest:

01

Royal Flush

A, K, Q, J, 10 of the same suit. The rarest and highest possible winning hand in poker.

02

Straight Flush

Five sequential cards of the same suit (e.g. 9-8-7-6-5 of spades).

03

Four of a Kind (Quads)

Four cards of the same rank (e.g. 4 Aces or 4 Kings) plus one kicker.

04

Full House (Boat)

Three cards of one rank combined with two cards of another rank (e.g. K-K-K-J-J).

05

Flush

Any five cards of the same suit, not in numerical sequence.

06

Straight

Five consecutive cards of differing suits (e.g. 8-7-6-5-4 of mixed suits).

07

Three of a Kind (Set / Trips)

Three cards of identical numerical rank (e.g. Q-Q-Q with two unrelated kickers).

08

Two Pair

Two distinct pairs of identical ranked cards (e.g. J-J and 8-8 with one side card).

09

One Pair

Two cards of matching rank (e.g. 10-10) accompanied by three kickers.

10

High Card

When no player creates a pair or better, the hand with the highest individual card wins (Ace high is strongest).
