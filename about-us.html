OUR MISSION & HERITAGE

THE 1ST LEGAL POKER HUB IN NCR

Club Re Straddle proudly holds the distinction of being the first fully certified and legal live poker club in NCR, situated at our luxury property in Sector 104, Noida. We operate under stringent legal safeguards, fair-play monitoring protocols, and international tournament standards.

Our core mission is to celebrate poker as an intellectual mind sport — offering an inviting haven for novices discovering the nuances of the game as well as high-stakes veterans seeking deep tournament competition and electrifying cash game action.

Equipped with custom RFID card tables, automated shuffling machines, certified croupiers, gourmet table-side dining, and private VIP lounges, Club Re Straddle sets an extraordinary standard for live gaming in India.
