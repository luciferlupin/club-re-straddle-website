THE LEGALITY OF PLAYING ON CLUB RE STRADDLE

Game of Skill Recognized Under Indian Law

Poker is recognized in Indian jurisprudence as a game of skill, and playing poker offline, whether for stakes or tournament trophies, is legitimate under Indian law. Engaging in skill-based games for monetary rewards is legally recognized as a protected business/recreational activity safeguarded under Article 19(1)(g) of the Constitution of India.

Supreme Court Judicial Precedent

In the landmark judgment K.R. Lakshmanan v. State of Tamil Nadu (AIR 1996 SC 1153), the Hon’ble Supreme Court of India established the definitive "Dominant Factor Test": a game is classified as one of skill when the element of player skill, strategy, probability calculation, and psychological judgment outweighs mere chance.

High Court Rulings on Poker

Multiple state High Courts across India, including the Karnataka High Court and the Allahabad High Court, have repeatedly recognized that poker requires substantial intellectual faculties, mathematical analysis, and disciplined decision-making, ruling that poker card rooms are not gaming houses or gambling dens when operated transparently.
