Introduction

This Privacy Notice informs you about how Club Re Straddle processes and protects your Personal Data and Information when you use our website, mobile application, or visit our offline club premises at JB Complex, Sector 104, Noida.

Information We Collect

To maintain strict membership security and comply with statutory KYC requirements, we collect identification details including full name, phone number, email address, government photo identification, and attendance logs.

Data Protection & Security

We implement rigorous administrative, physical, and technical safeguards complying with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), 2023. Your data is strictly kept confidential and never sold to third-party marketing entities.
