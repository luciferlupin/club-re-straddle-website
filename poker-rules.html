THE FUNDAMENTALS OF TEXAS HOLD'EM

Poker is a card game often considered a mind sport. While it seems complex on paper, it becomes intuitive, highly engaging, and straightforward to play live on our tables. Poker demands deep concentration, emotional control, and strategic thinking.

In Texas Hold’em, the objective is to make the best possible five-card poker hand using any combination of your two private hole cards and the five shared community cards on the board.

PLAYER ACTIONS

OPTIONS AVAILABLE DURING BETTING ROUNDS

Check

Passing the action to the next player without wagering chips, when no bet has been made in the current round.

Bet

Setting the wager amount when no previous player has bet in that specific round.

Call

Matching the exact bet or raise made by a prior opponent to stay active in the hand.

Raise

Increasing the size of the existing wager, requiring opponents to call more or fold.

Fold

Surrendering hole cards to the dealer, forfeiting all chips wagered in the pot for that hand.

Straddle & Re-Straddle

An optional blind bet made before cards are dealt, doubling the stakes and granting last action pre-flop — the signature tradition at Club Re Straddle!
