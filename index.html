ABOUT US

WHAT IS POKER

Poker is the ultimate sport of intellect, mathematics, and psychological warfare. Unlike games of sheer fortune, poker rewards disciplined decision-makers who calculate pot odds, analyze betting patterns, and outmaneuver their table adversaries.

Globally revered as a recognized mind sport, poker is celebrated across the world and protected under Indian constitutional jurisprudence as a game of skill. At Club Re Straddle, we elevate this classic sport into a world-class luxury club experience, uniting fair play, transparent oversight, and supreme hospitality in a sophisticated setting.
