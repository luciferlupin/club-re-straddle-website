Poker & Rummy Are Games Of Skill, Not Gambling: Allahabad HC Orders Reconsideration Of Gaming License

In a watershed legal verdict, the Division Bench of the Hon’ble Allahabad High Court reaffirmed that poker and rummy games depend predominantly upon analytical prowess, player psychology, and statistical probability rather than luck. The court directed state authorities to process club applications under the protected framework of legitimate commercial trade.

August 1, 2025 • High Court Rulings

Karnataka High Court Rules Poker as a Game of Skill, Quashes FIR Against Gaming Club

Reinforcing decades of Supreme Court jurisprudence stemming from the landmark K.R. Lakshmanan precedent, the Karnataka High Court firmly prohibited law enforcement interference in registered poker establishments that operate games of skill for recreational and tournament competition.

This decision further cements the constitutional rights of poker enthusiasts and club operators across the nation.
